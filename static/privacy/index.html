Privacy Notice

Last updated: June 2026

DocPass is a pilot platform. It is not an official NHS or NHS England system, and is not connected to NHS national systems. Acceptance policies for any training record remain those of each employer.

A. Who we are

DocPass is provided by DocPass Ltd as a pilot platform. It is operated separately from the NHS and is not endorsed by, or part of, NHS England, NHS Scotland, NHS Wales, NHS Northern Ireland, or any individual NHS Trust.

If you have a question about this notice or how your data is handled, contact us at privacy@docpass.co.uk.

DocPass Ltd acts as both a data controller and a data processor depending on the context of processing.

B. What data we collect

We collect only the information needed to run the training wallet and to support HR verification.

Account data

  • Name
  • Email address
  • GMC or NMC professional registration number
  • Professional role (for example, doctor)
  • Current NHS Trust

Training data

  • Training module names
  • Completion dates
  • Expiry dates
  • Training provider

Uploads

  • Certificates that you choose to attach (PDF or image)

System data

  • HR verification status for each record (for example, verified, pending, declined)
  • Messages exchanged between you and your Trust HR team inside DocPass

C. How we use your data

We use the information above to:

  • Provide your personal training wallet
  • Let you share records with your NHS Trust HR team for verification
  • Enable compliance checking of your records against your Trust’s mandatory training requirements
  • Support trust onboarding workflows (for example, when you move between Trusts)
  • Improve the reliability and usability of the platform

Matching of your records against Trust requirements uses a combination of rule-based matching (exact module codes, alias lists, and partial matches) and semantic matching of training topic names. See section G for what is sent for semantic matching.

D. Lawful basis

We process your personal data under the UK GDPR on the following bases:

  • Article 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract. We need to process your data to provide the DocPass service that you are using.
  • Article 6(1)(f) — legitimate interests. We process limited data to keep the platform secure, prevent fraud, and improve the system. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we balance these against your rights and interests.

We do not rely on consent as the primary lawful basis for running the platform.

E. Special category data

DocPass is not designed to process special category data (such as health information about patients, or information about your own health).

Please do not upload sensitive or clinical information, and do not include patient-identifiable data in any field or message.

There is a low residual risk that special category information could enter the platform through:

  • Free-text content that you type into messages
  • Certificates or other files you choose to upload

We mitigate this by clear guidance to users and by limiting how this content is used and shared.

F. How your data is shared

1. With NHS Trust HR teams

Your training records and the evidence you attach are shared with NHS Trust HR teams only when you choose to share them (for example, by sending records for verification or sharing a verified pack when moving Trusts). HR users at your Trust then see the records you have shared so they can verify, decline, or comment on them.

2. With our sub-processors

To run the platform, DocPass uses the following sub-processors. Each handles only the data they need for their specific role:

  • Self-hosted infrastructure (UK) — hosting and storage of the DocPass application and database on DocPass-operated hardware (Raspberry Pi)
  • Resend (EU) — transactional email delivery (for example, sign-in codes, password reset links, HR notifications)
  • Google Cloud — semantic matching of training topic names (see section G)

G. AI / automated processing

DocPass uses a semantic matching model (provided by Google Cloud) to help compare training topic names that do not match through rule-based matching alone. This is important for explainability and consistency, not for making decisions about individuals.

What we send for semantic matching:
  • Only the training topic name and the module title being compared
  • No personal data is sent
  • No identifiers (no name, email, GMC/NMC number, user ID, Trust)
  • No free-text content from messages or other fields

No personal data, identifiers, or free-text content is transmitted.

The data sent is not used to train external models. Semantic matching only suggests possible matches — HR users remain responsible for verification decisions, and you remain responsible for what is in your wallet.

H. Data storage and retention

  • Your data is stored on UK-based self-hosted infrastructure (DocPass-operated Raspberry Pi).
  • It is stored securely behind authentication and access controls (see section J).
  • We retain your data while your account is active and you continue to use the platform.
  • If you delete your account, your data is deleted within 30 days.
  • Encrypted backups may persist for a short additional period before they roll out of the backup window.

I. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@docpass.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

J. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS for all traffic between your browser and DocPass
  • Secure authentication, including password hashing, session cookies that are not accessible to JavaScript, and an email-based second factor for selected HR accounts
  • Access controls so that doctors only see their own wallet, and HR users only see records that have been shared with their Trust
  • Routine patching, restricted server access, and monitoring of the production environment

No service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk to a level appropriate for a pilot system handling training records.

K. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time — for example, when we add new features, change a sub-processor, or improve clarity. Material changes will be reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

Please check this page periodically. The version published here is always the current version.
