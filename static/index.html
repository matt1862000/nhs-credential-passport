Portable, HR‑verified mandatory training records for NHS staff.

Training Passport is a focused solution for mandatory/statutory training evidence: store records in one place, capture HR verification, control visibility, and map completions to destination trust requirements.

Create an account Sign in

How it works

  1. Sign in or create an account, then complete your profile
  2. Add training manually or import from ESR (with supporting evidence)
  3. HR verifies your records; you choose visibility, plan a trust move against checklist packs, and message HR when needed

Built for clinicians moving trusts

Keep mandatory training evidence in one place, get HR verification when needed, and reuse verified completions when you change employer.

What you can do

  • Add training or import from ESR (with evidence)
  • Send items to HR for verification
  • Control visibility so permitted HR can find your verified training
  • Plan a trust move against destination checklist packs
  • Message HR (including supported other trusts)
What this covers (and what it doesn’t) Pre‑employment checks (DBS, right to work, OH) stay local.

Features

  • ESR CSV import with evidence upload
  • HR verification workflow (verified / declined)
  • Messages with your trust HR and supported other trusts
  • Trust move planner with checklist packs
  • Download HR‑verified evidence PDFs (ZIP export)

Pilot demonstration service—not an official NHS national passport. Acceptance policies remain those of each employer.
