Portable, HR‑verified mandatory training records for NHS doctors.

DocPass is a proof-of-concept demonstrating how mandatory training evidence could be stored in one place, reviewed through an HR-style verification process, and compared against destination trust requirements. The concept is designed in line with the direction of NHS England’s Statutory and Mandatory Training (StatMand) programme, which aims to reduce duplication and enable greater portability of training across organisations.

Across the NHS, there is increasing focus on reducing repeated mandatory training and enabling staff to move more easily between organisations. National frameworks such as the Core Skills Training Framework (CSTF) aim to standardise training so it can be recognised across employers. DocPass explores how a clinician-held training passport, combined with HR verification, could support this model in practice.

How it works

  1. Sign in or create an account, then complete your profile
  2. Add training manually or import from ESR (with supporting evidence)
  3. HR verifies your records; you choose visibility, plan a trust move against checklist packs, and message HR when needed

Built for doctors moving trusts

How it helps

  • Keep your mandatory training in one place, instead of re-sending the same evidence repeatedly
  • Get HR to confirm what they’ve checked, so your record is clearer when you move
  • Let permitted HR find your verified training, without you chasing email threads
  • See what your next trust might ask for, before your start date
  • Align with emerging NHS approaches to standardising mandatory training (e.g. CSTF) and reducing duplication
  • Contact HR directly when you need a decision or clarification

Included

  • Import from ESR or add training manually
  • Attach supporting evidence (PDF or screenshot)
  • HR review with a clear outcome: verified or declined
  • Download a ZIP of your HR‑verified evidence PDFs
  • Checklist packs for supported destination trusts
  • Messaging with your trust HR (and supported other trusts)
