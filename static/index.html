Moving trusts

Plan your move

Follow the four steps below in order. Start with an account so ESR imports can be saved to the service; then bring your ESR export into this app; then compare your list to your new trust’s checklist. Your new trust’s HR and Medical Staffing always decide final recognition.

  1. 1

    Create an account or sign in

    Use a work email you can access. When you are signed in, your training list is saved to this service as well as in your browser, so CSV imports from the next step stay with your account on this device. If your trust does not allow accounts here, you can still continue using file backup only — but you will not get server sync.

  2. 2

    Export from ESR and import CSV

    In ESR (Electronic Staff Record), open the area your trust uses for learning and compliance — for example Compliance and Competency — and export the data you need, usually as a CSV file. Stay in this same browser (so your sign-in from step 1 still applies), then open My e-learning → Import CSV. Run Check CSV, then Issue all valid rows. Your completions appear under My e-learning and, when signed in, sync to your account.

    Optional: If you already saved a JSON backup from My e-learning on another device, you can bring it here instead of or as well as CSV — same browser as step 1.

    Sign in at step 1 to unlock ESR import and JSON upload.

    From My e-learning: Save or restore your list → Save a copy to my device on the other device, then choose the file here.
  3. 3

    Choose trusts and load the checklist

    Pick where you are joining and leaving. Then run the checklist so we can load that destination’s mandatory topics (UK CSTF–aligned where the pack lists them).

    Sign in and finish step 2 first, then choose trusts and run this checklist.

    Review gaps, recognition, and what to do next
