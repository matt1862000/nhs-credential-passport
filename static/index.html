Verified NHS mandatory training you can carry—not another full onboarding from scratch.

National programmes for sharing all staff credentials between trusts are still evolving; many clinicians still re-prove the same learning every time they move. Training Passport focuses on the slice employers actually re-check often: mandatory and statutory training—stored once, verified by HR, visible under your control, and mapped to your next trust’s requirements.

How it works

  1. Sign in or create an account, then complete your profile
  2. Add training manually or import from ESR (with supporting evidence)
  3. HR verifies your records; you choose visibility, plan a trust move against checklist packs, and message HR when needed

What this solves today—and what it doesn’t

Commentary on NHS workforce mobility often highlights the gap left when narrow “digital passport” pilots retire or stall: receiving trusts still need assurance, while clinicians repeat paperwork. Partial portability—especially around pre-employment checks—does not remove the need to evidence ongoing mandatory training. Training Passport is built for that ongoing layer: a structured wallet, HR attestation, and tools so you and authorised HR can reuse verified completions instead of starting from zero each time.

In Training Passport

  • One place for completions and evidence (add or ESR import)
  • Share with HR for verification; audit-friendly decisions on what was checked
  • Visibility you control so permitted HR can discover verified training
  • Plan a trust move: compare your wallet to pilot destination checklist packs
  • Message HR—including another trust’s HR where the service supports it

Still with Medical Staffing / HR locally

  • Pre-employment checks (e.g. DBS, right to work, occupational health, references)
  • Professional registration and revalidation with regulators
  • Trust induction, IT access, safeguarding pathways, and specialty competencies
  • Final acceptance of any credential always rests with the receiving organisation

Pilot demonstration service—not an official NHS national passport. Requirements and acceptance policies remain those of each employer; use this to prepare evidence and conversations, not as a substitute for local compliance processes.
