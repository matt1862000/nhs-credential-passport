      How to export your training from ESR

      Follow these steps in the ESR portal. Labels can vary slightly by role or ESR version.

      1. Log into the ESR Portal.
      2. Scroll down to Compliance and expand the menu (down arrow).
      3. Click View My Compliance (your own record — not a team list).
      4. Click Export.
      5. Save the CSV, then take a screenshot or export a PDF from ESR as supporting evidence.
      6. Continue through the steps below to preview and import.

      We matched your CSV columns automatically. Check the summary below, then continue — you only need to change something if a field looks wrong.
