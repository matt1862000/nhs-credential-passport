Verify training

Check that a staff member’s training record is valid. Paste the training credential ID or the verification URL that contains a jwt= parameter (from their credential link).

Do not paste links to /static/hr/?session=… here — those are for HR to open the HR inbox while signed in, not for this checker.

If the staff member shared a link, paste the entire URL here.

Import (multiple training records)

If the staff member shared one link or QR for several training records (e.g. Fire Safety and Information Governance), paste that bundle URL here to verify them all at once.
