What DocPass covers

DocPass focuses on mandatory and statutory training evidence: collecting records and evidence in one place, supporting HR verification, and helping you reuse verified completions when you move trusts. It is not a full replacement for local onboarding and pre-employment checks.

In DocPass

  • One place for completions and evidence (add or ESR import)
  • Send records to HR for verification
  • Visibility controls so permitted HR can discover verified training
  • Plan a trust move: compare your wallet to destination checklist packs (where available)
  • Message HR, including other trusts supported on the service

Still local to each employer

  • Pre-employment checks (DBS, right to work, occupational health, references)
  • Professional registration and revalidation with regulators
  • Trust induction, IT access, safeguarding pathways, specialty competencies
  • Final acceptance of any credential always rests with the receiving organisation

docpass.co.uk — pilot demonstration service; not an official NHS national passport. Requirements and acceptance policies remain those of each employer.
